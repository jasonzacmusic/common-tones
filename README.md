# Common Tones

Practice app for the Common Tones lesson by Jason Zac (Nathaniel School of Music):
the chords one, two and three fingers away from home, in all 12 keys, major, natural minor and
harmonic minor, triads and seventh chords, with real piano playback down to 40 bpm, a metronome,
and a "which note moved?" ear trainer.

Live at https://common-tones.nathanielschool.com

`index.html` is the self-contained output of `source/build_app.py`, built in
`~/Documents/NSM Lessons/Common Tones 20 Oct 2026/` (that folder also holds the cached fonts and samples).
Deploy: `vercel deploy --prod` from this folder (git deploys are off).
